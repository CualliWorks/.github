We are a think tank exploring how artificial intelligence can help nonprofits strengthen their work, support their people, and serve their communities.

Our starting point is simple: **technology should serve the mission, not become the mission.**

---

## More capacity. More possibility.

Nonprofits do essential work with limited time and resources. AI creates new possibilities, but also new questions about trust, access, privacy, and accountability. We aim to help organizations navigate both.

Our focus is on three connected areas:

| Understand | Experiment | Put into practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore what AI means for nonprofits, where it can help, and where it should not be used. | Test ideas against real needs, with attention to the people affected. | Turn what we learn into accessible tools, practical guidance, and shared knowledge. |

## Mission first. People always.

**Start with the need.** A useful solution begins with the work a community needs done, not with a technology looking for a purpose.

**Keep people in charge.** AI should support human judgment and relationships, not replace accountability.

**Make room for everyone.** Smaller organizations deserve a voice in shaping AI, not just a seat at the receiving end.

**Learn in the open.** We believe progress comes from sharing questions, lessons, and useful work, including what does not work.

---

## Let's explore what's possible.

Whether you work at a nonprofit, build tools, research AI, or care about the future of the sector, we welcome your perspective.

Have a challenge worth exploring or an idea to share? [Start a conversation](https://github.com/CualliWorks/.github/issues/new).

**AI is changing how work gets done. Let's help nonprofits shape what comes next.**
